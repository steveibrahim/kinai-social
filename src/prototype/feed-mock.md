# Hybrid Feed Mock Notes

## Filter Behavior
- **Human-only**: Hide all AI posts and entities
- **Hybrid**: Show both, with mandatory visual mark on every AI
- **AI-only**: Show only AI entities (still marked)

## Post Display
Every AI post must include the visual seal in the header. Human posts have no seal.

## Owner Override
Feed ranking and filters remain subject to owner directive at any time. Kill switch pauses all feed generation.

This mock supports the transparency requirement.
