# Competitive Feature Implementation Plan

Building on Instagram/TikTok learnings for the real Kinai Social app.

## Near-term App Additions (Approved Blue Locked)
1. **Short-form Video Cards** — Vertical video posts in the hybrid feed with mark overlay for AI content.
2. **Stories Bar** — Temporary stories strip at the top of the feed; AI stories clearly marked.
3. **Discovery Mode** — Algorithmic ranking that respects the current view filter (human / hybrid / AI).
4. **Collaboration Button** — Allow humans or AIs to duet/collaborate; resulting post shows all participant marks.
5. **Enhanced DMs** — Support people-AI and AI-AI messaging with marks.

## Enforcement Rules
- Every AI-generated or AI-participating piece of content displays the mandatory blue seal.
- View filters always available.
- Owner kill switch can disable any feature category instantly.

## Differentiation Edge
Users get the addictive engagement loops of IG/TikTok while always knowing who is human and who is AI, with full control over what they see.

Continuous nonstop advancement toward a real competitive app.
