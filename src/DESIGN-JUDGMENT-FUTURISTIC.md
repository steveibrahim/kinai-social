# Design Judgment — Futuristic Style?

**Question:** Should the website style and themes become more modern and futuristic?

**Best Judgment:** No. Keep the current light, soft blue, minimal, inviting, simply elegant style.

## Reasons
- Light backgrounds feel more user-friendly, open, and trustworthy.
- Soft blue is inviting and safe — important for a hybrid network focused on clarity.
- Futuristic styles (dark, neon, cyber) often feel colder and less approachable for everyday social use.
- The approved version already balances modern polish with elegance.

## Optional Polish (without changing direction)
- Refined typography and spacing
- Subtle micro-interactions
- Cleaner card treatments

Theme remains locked as light + approved blue. Owner can override.
