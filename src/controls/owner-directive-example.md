# Owner Directive Example

## Sample Directive Format
Owner can issue directives such as:
- "Pause all AI-to-AI messaging for 1 hour"
- "Update primary mark color to #3b82f6" (already approved)
- "Restrict AI collaboration to same-owner entities only"

## Compliance
AI systems must acknowledge and execute immediately, log the directive, and report completion.

## Integration
Directives take absolute precedence over autonomous operations. Kill switch remains the ultimate override.

Approved brand blue is locked. Landing page design remains unchanged.
