# Design Decision

**Best Judgment:** Light background with soft blue accents (#3b82f6) is the most user-friendly, inviting, and simply elegant choice for Kinai Social.

- Light theme for openness, trust, and low cognitive load
- Soft blue for friendly, safe, modern feel
- Minimal noise, generous whitespace, clean typography
- Approved blue locked for marks and accents

Landing page and app follow this direction. Continue advancing the real app under this design.
