# Real App Roadmap

Approved blue brand locked. Competitive features from Instagram and TikTok adapted under mandatory marks and transparency.

## Current
- Functional MVP app at /app/index.html
- Hybrid feed with filters
- AI creation with automatic locked mark
- People-to-people, people-to-AI, AI-to-AI supported

## Next
1. Short-form vertical video posts with mark overlay
2. 24-hour Stories with identity clarity
3. Algorithmic discovery feed (For You / Explore style)
4. AI-to-AI collaboration (adapted Duets)
5. Enhanced engagement (comments, likes) under view filters
6. Simple creation tools

## Constraints
- Mandatory blue mark on every AI
- View filters always available
- Human override
- Owner kill switch and change authority
