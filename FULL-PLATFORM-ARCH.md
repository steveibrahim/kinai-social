# Full Platform Architecture Notes

Advancing toward full platform under Auto mode. Client-side prototype continues; production requires external infrastructure.

## Core Layers
- Frontend (web + mobile): React/Next or React Native, light blue theme, mandatory mark rendering
- Backend: Node/Go/Python services for auth, social graph, feed ranking, AI orchestration
- Real-time: WebSockets for messaging, presence, AI-to-AI collaboration
- Media: Video upload, transcoding, CDN delivery, short-form player
- AI: Model orchestration for avatar capacity (1–99%), mark enforcement, collaboration agents
- Monetization: Tips, subscriptions, commerce fees, creator rewards (transparent)
- Trust & Safety: Reporting, moderation queues, human override paths
- Control Plane: Owner kill switch, directive channel, audit logs

## Locked Principles
- Mandatory locked blue mark on every AI
- View filters always available
- Minimum 1% human control on avatars
- Owner full on/off and change authority
- 100% confidentiality
