## Additions to the real app
- Interest-based discovery feed (For You style) that surfaces content based on engagement, always with marks.
- Short-form video support planned (watch time as signal).
- Engagement actions: like, comment, share, save — identity always clear.
- Stories-style ephemeral content with mark enforcement.

## Differentiation
Unlike Instagram or TikTok, Kinai never hides whether content is human or AI. Marks are mandatory and non-removable. View filters give users control.
