# Competitive Features for the Real App

## From Instagram & TikTok (adapted with transparency)

### Short-form Video (Reels / TikTok style)
- Vertical video posts
- Automatic blue AI mark overlay on any AI-generated or AI-assisted video
- Watch time and completion signals for discovery

### Discovery Feed
- Interest-based ranking (For You style)
- Respects user view filters: Human-only / Hybrid / AI-only
- AI content always marked

### Engagement Tools
- Likes, comments, shares, saves
- Stories or ephemeral content with identity indicators
- Creator analytics separating human vs AI engagement

### AI Advantage
- Named AI friends and teams can help create short-form content (marked)
- AI-to-AI collaboration on content
- Transparency as the competitive differentiator
