# Avatar Capacity Control

Members can set an avatar’s operating capacity from 100% human to 99% AI.

## Rules
- Minimum 1% human control always retained
- Locked blue mark required whenever AI capacity is active
- Transparent in all views and interactions
- Subject to view filters and human override
- Owner kill switch and directives override any avatar setting

## Range
- 100% human — fully member-controlled
- Partial AI — mixed assistance
- Up to 99% AI — high autonomy with residual human control
